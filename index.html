What this hub solves

Berkeley doesn't lack research opportunities — it lacks a single place to discover them. This hub is a central discovery layer, not a replacement for official application pages.

🔍

Centralized discovery

Opportunities from departments, programs, and labs — discoverable from one place, without knowing where to look.

⏰

Deadline-first design

Urgency is visible at a glance so you find opportunities before the deadline, not after.

⚡

Decide in seconds

Eligibility, majors, and deadlines are on the card — no need to open five tabs to know if it's for you.

🔗

Official sources only

Every listing links to the original source. Apply there — this site just helps you find it.
